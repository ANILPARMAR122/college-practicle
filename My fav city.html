My Favourite City: New York City

The City That Never Sleeps

New York City, located in the southeastern part of New York State, is the most populous city in the United States. Renowned as a global hub for finance, art, theater, fashion, and culture, it is famous for its iconic skyline, world-class museums, diverse neighborhoods, and vibrant energy.

Famous Places to Visit

  • Statue of Liberty
  • Central Park
  • Empire State Building
  • Times Square
  • Brooklyn Bridge

Top Activities for Visitors

  1. Watch a world-famous musical or theater show on Broadway.
  2. Take a scenic walk or bicycle ride through Central Park.
  3. Enjoy panoramic views of the skyline from the Empire State Building observatory.

Place Details

1. Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty is a colossal neoclassical sculpture on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. Dedicated in 1886 as a gift from France, it stands as an enduring universal symbol of freedom and democracy.

↑ Back to top

2. Central Park

Central Park is an expansive 843-acre urban park located in the center of Manhattan. It features scenic walking paths, artificial lakes, the Central Park Zoo, and open recreational fields.

↑ Back to top

3. Empire State Building

The Empire State Building is a famous 102-story Art Deco skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan. It is well known for its towering spire and open-air observation deck that offers panoramic views.

↑ Back to top

4. Times Square

Times Square is a major commercial intersection and entertainment hub in Midtown Manhattan, globally recognized for its massive illuminated billboards, theaters, and vibrant nightlife.

↑ Back to top

5. Brooklyn Bridge

The Brooklyn Bridge spans the East River, connecting Manhattan and Brooklyn. Opened in 1883, this historic suspension bridge offers a dedicated pedestrian promenade with skyline views.
